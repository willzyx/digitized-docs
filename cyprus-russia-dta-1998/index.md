# Cyprus–Russia agreement on the avoidance of double taxation

Agreement between the Government of the Republic of Cyprus and the Government of the Russian
Federation for the avoidance of double taxation with respect to taxes on income and on capital.

**[English text](en.md)** · **[Russian text](ru.md)** · [original scan, PDF 23 MB](https://github.com/willzyx/digitized-docs/releases/download/cyprus-russia-dta-1998/cyprus-russia-dta-1998.pdf)

## The document

| | |
|---|---|
| Parties | the Republic of Cyprus and the Russian Federation |
| Signed | 5 December 1998 at Nicosia |
| Original languages | Greek, Russian, English — all texts equally authentic |
| In case of divergence | the English text governs (per the agreement's closing formula) |
| Structure | 30 articles |
| Supersedes | the Cyprus–USSR convention on the avoidance of double taxation of 29 October 1982 |

The Greek text is not part of this edition; only the English and the Russian appear.

## Source

Official publication of the Republic of Cyprus, pages 117–170. The scan came as a PDF (original
filename `Russiaenglisg.pdf`, 54 pages, Adobe Acrobat 6.02, 2006). It carries no text layer:
`pdftotext` returns 54 bytes, all of them newlines.

The pages in that PDF are **out of order**, which is worth knowing for anyone working from the same
file:

| Part | Pages of the edition | Pages of the PDF |
|---|---|---|
| English text | 117–141 | 1–25, in order |
| Russian text | 142–169 | 53, 52, 51 … 26 — reversed |
| Russian text, final page | 170 | 54 |

Read the PDF straight through and the Russian half runs backwards.

## How it was digitized

Pages were rendered to 300 dpi PNG and put through Tesseract 4.1.1 (`-l eng` and `-l rus`,
`--psm 6`), after which all 54 pages were proofread by hand against their images.

Checks run on the assembled text: both versions carry exactly 30 articles, the numbering is
unbroken, and the article headings line up in pairs. Every figure was verified separately — the
dividend rates (5% where the beneficial owner has invested in the company's capital no less than the
equivalent of 100,000 US dollars, 10% otherwise), the 183-day and twelve-month thresholds, the
five-year and two-year periods, and the dates 29 October 1982 and 5 December 1998.

## Typos in the original

Preserved as printed, and flagged in the text with an HTML comment:

| Where | As printed | Presumably intended |
|---|---|---|
| Article 3(2), English | `defind` | defined |
| Article 5(4), English | `permanent stablishment` | permanent establishment |
| Article 5(5), English | `on behlaf of` | on behalf of |
| Article 10(5), English | `unistributed profits` | undistributed profits |
| Article 25(2), Russian | «положениям настоящего Соглашению» | a case-agreement error: настоящего Соглашения |

One divergence between the two versions is not a typo and cannot be resolved as one: in Article
11(3) the Russian text says the provisions of paragraphs 1 **and 2** shall not apply, while the
English says only paragraph 1.

In both texts the place and date of signature are handwritten in the original.
